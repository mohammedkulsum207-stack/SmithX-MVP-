    BUILD LOG

    SMITHX Updates

    Follow the evolution of the SMITHX MVP.

    Marketplace foundation

    Product discovery and marketplace browsing added.

    Shopping cart

    Cart management and demo checkout added.

    Seller Studio

    Sellers can create and publish products.

    AI Product Assistant

    AI listing generation interface added.

    Order Management

    Demo orders can now be created, viewed and managed through Pending, Confirmed, Shipped and Delivered statuses.

    Customer Order Tracking

    Dedicated customer order history and delivery tracking experience added.

    Seller Storefronts

    Sellers can create a store with its own name, link, colour theme and description, then preview, publish and share it.

    Marketplace upgrade

    Sort and price filters, a saved-products wishlist, shareable product links and a mobile navigation menu.

    Account improvements

    Password reset and country selection added to accounts.

    Full checkout

    Delivery address, delivery options with estimated dates, demo M-Pesa and card payment, and a full price breakdown before you pay.

    Cancellations, refunds and receipts

    Cancel before shipping, request a return after delivery, print receipts, and keep delivered orders in your history.

    SMX Credit wallet (demo)

    Top up with M-Pesa, pay for all or part of an order with SMX, get refunds straight back to your wallet. 1 SMX = KES 1.

    Auctions, reviews and Store Assistant

    Bid on items (losing bids are free), rate and review what you bought, and get store design ideas in one click.

    International shopping and insights

    See prices in your currency, choose Kiswahili, join the waitlist for your country, and comfortable on every screen.
