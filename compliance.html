Compliance & Prohibited Items

SmithX is a marketplace. Sellers are responsible for the legality, accuracy, safety, and quality of the products they list.

International transactions are not yet live. Country-specific payment support, international shipping, and cross-border compliance must be completed before SmithX enables them.

Supported market

SmithX currently applies marketplace rules for Kenya. Sellers must be at least 18 years old and provide valid contact details. Identity or business verification is required before seller payouts.

Prohibited items

The following may not be listed, sold, or promoted on SmithX:

  • Illegal drugs and controlled substances
  • Weapons, ammunition, and explosive devices
  • Counterfeit or unauthorised branded goods
  • Stolen goods
  • Wildlife products and protected-species items
  • Human remains, body parts, or bodily fluids
  • Hate speech, terrorist, or extremist material
  • Sexual services or explicit sexual content
  • Unlicensed gambling or financial services

Restricted items

Some products may be listed only when the seller meets applicable requirements:

  • Alcohol: Applicable licences and age restrictions apply.
  • Medicines and health products: Authorisation and required product information apply.
  • Electronics with batteries: Safety and transport requirements apply.
  • Food and consumables: Expiry, storage, and compliance information applies.

Reporting a listing

If you see a counterfeit, unsafe, misleading, prohibited, or restricted listing, report it through the listing report option or contact SmithX support. SmithX may remove listings and suspend accounts that violate these rules.
