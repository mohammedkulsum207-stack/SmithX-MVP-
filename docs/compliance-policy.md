# SmithX Compliance Policy

Language: English.

## Current status

SmithX currently supports Kenya as the initial compliance market. International transactions are not enabled.

Live international transactions require completion of:

1. A production backend with shared buyer and seller data.
2. Authentication, email or phone verification, and account recovery.
3. Payment-provider integration, seller payouts, and reconciliation.
4. Courier integration and shipment tracking.
5. Country-specific legal, tax, consumer-protection, and data-protection review.
6. Return, refund, dispute, moderation, and reporting workflows.

## Kenya

### Seller eligibility

- Sellers must be at least 18 years old.
- Sellers must provide a valid email address and phone number.
- Identity or business verification is required before payouts.
- Accepted verification documents include a National ID, passport, or business registration certificate.
- Sellers must provide accurate listing, stock, price, delivery, and return information.

### Prohibited products

- Illegal drugs and controlled substances
- Weapons, ammunition, and explosive devices
- Counterfeit or unauthorised branded goods
- Stolen goods
- Wildlife products and protected-species items
- Human remains, body parts, or bodily fluids
- Hate speech, terrorist, or extremist material
- Sexual services or explicit sexual content
- Gambling services requiring an unlicensed operator
- Financial products requiring an unlicensed provider

### Restricted products

- **Alcohol:** Applicable licences and age restrictions are required.
- **Medicines and health products:** Authorisation and required product information are required.
- **Electronics with batteries:** Safety and transport requirements apply.
- **Food and consumables:** Expiry, storage, and applicable compliance information is required.

## Enforcement

SmithX may remove listings, suspend accounts, withhold payouts pending review, and report suspected illegal activity where required by law.

## Reporting

Buyers and sellers should be able to report counterfeit, unsafe, misleading, prohibited, or restricted listings. Reports must be reviewed through the future admin and moderation workflow.
